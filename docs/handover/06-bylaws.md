## Buildings other than houses: CDA, DHA Islamabad, LDA (1.45)

In `src/lib/buildingBylaws.ts`, every figure with its clause; plan `mimar-1.45/plan.md` in the project files has the
tables in full. A Building project on a plot under one of these picks an **area** (Plot panel), and the plan check
measures the area's limits and the authority's facilities. Parking is counted from rooms named Parking, Car park or
Garage at 250 sq ft a car (CDA 8.1(9), DHA 6.8.12; LDA gives only the 8'×16' stall, so Mimar uses 250 too).

- **CDA** (ICT Building Control Regulations 2020 as amended by S.R.O. 1074(I)/2023, Section 8): 21 areas, 8.1 Blue
  Area north (FAR 1:8/1:9/1:10 by size, 70%, 7' veranda) to 8.23 halls (1:1, 50%, 30'/15'/15'/15', 2,000 sq yd,
  100' front, 60' road). Markaz 1:5 up to 899 sq yd (LG+G+5 or G+6), 1:6 from 900 (LG+G+7 or G+8), 6' veranda.

## Mumty and car porch (1.23)

- **CDA (sectors), Schedule-I:** mumty up to ⅓ of the ground floor's permissible block (the block left by the
  setbacks) on plots up to 200 sq yd, ¼ above; height up to 10' in houses (2.14.1). The 30' building height is taken
  without the mumty.
- **Islamabad private schemes, Schedule-5:** mumty 200 / 350 / 400 sq ft, but which plot sizes these go with could not
  be read for certain, so only the 10' height (2.14.1) is checked.
- **DHA Islamabad & Rawalpindi:** mumty area 8.57.5 as a share of the plot, from the row at or below its size: 125 sq
  yd 20%, 200 13.5%, 250–400 11%, 500–1000 9% (e.g. 500 sq yd → 405 sq ft); height 11' from the first-floor roof slab
  (8.57.3); width at most half the plot's average width (8.57.2); counted in the 37' height (8.56.3). Car porch
  largest size including the side setback (8.56.10.1): up to 200 sq yd 14×18'; 201–399 20×18'; 400–800 30×35';
  above 800 32×35'; a second porch (18×18' or smaller by size) only on corner plots or plots opening on two roads.

In the plan, the mumty is a floor above the ground named Roof, Mumty or Stair tower (or holding a room so named); car
porches are ground-floor rooms named Porch or Garage.

## Corner plots (1.30)

No official corner-plot figures yet. A plot with two road sides uses the front setback on its main road and the
side setback (side 1 or side 2, by position) on the second road, marked **provisional** in the Sides list and the
plan check, which adds a "Corner plot" row to check with the authority. The cut corner (splay) is off by default;
when ticked it starts at 5' along each road. Replace these with the authorities' figures when the owner uploads them.

## Every locality and every kind of building (1.43)

  too), with a "Local bylaws" row to pick the authority.
