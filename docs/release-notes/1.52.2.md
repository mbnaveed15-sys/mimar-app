## New in 1.52.2: the structure stays inside the building line, with a facade

- **Inside the building line.** Generate structure now keeps everything it makes inside the setbacks, so the plan check passes: columns on the outside walls sit flush with the walls' outer faces (a 12" column in a 9" wall stands out on the inside only), and footings and tie beams at the building line are moved in (eccentric footings) or cut back to it. Boundary walls' strip footings belong to the boundary wall and aren't counted as building in the setbacks.
- **Facade: plastered or exposed** (Structure panel). **Plastered**, the default, has the walls' finish run over the columns, beams and lintels in 3D and in the elevations, with no seams; columns and beams that stand out of a wall inside a room are plastered in that wall's colour. **Exposed** shows the concrete frame in the walls, as 1.52.1 did. Either way the facade adds no thickness, so it never reaches into the setbacks. "Only the structure in 3D" still shows the frame in concrete.
