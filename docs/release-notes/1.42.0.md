## New in 1.42.0: Checks your way, non-residential checks and more curves

- **One mode for everyone.** The Simple and Pro switch is gone: every tool is on the tool bars from the start.
- **A Checks button** at the bottom, next to Grid and Snap (or F4). Click it to turn all checks and their red and amber marks off, so you can sketch freely, and on again when you want them. Hover over it (or tap the small arrow) to turn each kind of check on or off: plot and setbacks, size and height, room sizes, basement, building use, layout and access, daylight and air, door and window gaps, and furniture space.
- **The Snap button works the same way.** Click it to turn snapping off (F3), or hover to choose which snaps you want: ends, midpoints, wall faces, crossings, perpendicular, on a wall or line, centres, the red and green axes, lining up with other points, the building line, and the door and window spacing marks.
- **X and Y of the pointer** show at the bottom while you draw, measured from the plan's origin.
- **Checks for non-residential buildings.** A Building project now gets checks for its use (offices, schools, clinics, shops and mosques): how many people each floor holds, the number of exits and their widths, stair and corridor widths, doors wide enough for a wheelchair, how far anyone has to walk to an exit, toilets and WCs for the number of people, an accessible toilet, a ramp at the entrance and a lift above the ground floor. They are good-practice figures, shown as hints, to check with the authority.
- **Windows in curved walls follow the curve:** bent in the plan and the DXF, and made of panes along the curve in 3D.
- **Curved and spiral stairs.** The stair maker has two new shapes. A **curved stair** winds round an inner radius you set; a **spiral stair** winds round a central pole. Both turn left or right, and the side panel warns when a spiral stair is too tight for headroom.
- **Curved edges.** Select a slab or a levelled area and drag the round grip in the middle of any edge to bend it into a curve ("Make edges straight" undoes it). **Plot sides** bend the same way: the boundary wall becomes a curved wall, and the setback line, plot area, frontage and plan check follow the curve.
- **Curved paths and driveways.** The Curve tool can now make a **Path or driveway** of the width you choose, along any curve: an arc, a circle (a ring round a lawn or fountain), an oval or a freeform curve. Paths lie on the ground and the plan check leaves them out, so they can run through the setbacks.
- Plan files move to format 13 (for curved and spiral stairs and curved edges). Older files open as before.
