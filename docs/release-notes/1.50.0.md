## New in 1.50.0: drawings, roofs, the sun and north, and handy extras

- **Dimensions on sections and elevations.** Each section and elevation gets a height string at the side (plinth, each floor, top of the walls, the ridge and the overall height). Sections also get a string under the cut, with wall thicknesses and clear spaces. Elevations get the overall width. A **Dimensions** switch in the Drawings view turns them off.
- **Hatching.** What a section cuts gets the usual hatch for its material (brick, concrete, stone, timber, earth, glass) instead of solid black. Choose **Hatched** or **Solid black** in the Drawings view.
- **Hatches on the plan.** Walls and columns get their cut hatch on the plan. Floors, lawns, paths and levelled areas get a surface hatch: grass, earth, gravel, sand, water, paving, concrete, tiles, timber, brick, stone, glass and more. Each material has a **Hatch** choice in the Materials panel, and Settings can turn plan hatches off. 3D keeps its materials.
- **Dimension look.** In Settings, pick the dimension **text size** (small, medium or large) and the **ends** (tick, arrow or dot). It applies on screen, in the PDF, on sheets and in the DXF, and is saved with the plan.
- **Real dimensions in the DXF.** Dimensions go out as DIMENSION entities, so AutoCAD, BricsCAD and LibreCAD can stretch and edit them. Hatches go on their own layers.
- **3D views on sheets.** The default sheets add a "3D views" sheet with an isometric line drawing and a coloured 3D picture.
- **Any scale for a sheet drawing.** Pick 1:20 to 1:500 from the list, or choose **Custom…** and type any scale, such as 1:60.
- **A pitch for each roof side.** Select a roof and give any side its own pitch. A blank side uses the roof's pitch, and the eaves stay level.
- **Skylight tool (Shift+K).** Click on a roof slope to put a roof window there (780 × 980 mm by default). It cuts the roof and adds a frame and glass. It shows on the plan, in 3D, in sections, the DXF, IFC and quantities.
- **Dormer tool (Shift+Y).** Click on a roof slope to add a dormer: a small box with a window in its front and its own gable or shed roof, 600 mm in from the eaves. Change its width, wall height and roof in the roof's panel.
- **The sun and shadows.** The 3D view has a **Sun** button. Turn shadows on or off, or use the real sun for a month, day and time at your site's latitude.
- **North arrow tool (Shift+N).** Click where the arrow goes, then click the way north lies. The arrow shows on the plan, on sheets and in the DXF, and north turns the sun in 3D.
- **Layout lines split solids.** A straight layout line drawn right across a block or slab splits it, so each piece can be pushed or pulled on its own.
- **Divide.** Select a wall or layout line and divide it into equal parts, or into pieces of a set length. The new ends snap like any others.
- **Open recent (Ctrl+Shift+O).** File › Open recent… lists the last plans you opened or saved. The welcome screen has it too.
- **Room areas in sq ft, marla or both.** Rooms on the plan now show sq ft only. Settings › **Room areas in** can show marla, or both.
- **Properties comes forward.** Picking a tool turns the side panel to Properties, so its settings are in front of you.
- **Fixed:** undoing right after **Duplicate this floor above** (or adding a floor) crashed the app. It now goes back to a floor that is still there.
- Plan files move to format 20. Older files open as before.
