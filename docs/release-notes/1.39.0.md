## New in 1.39.0: movable side panels

- **Put the panels in any order.** Drag the grip on a section heading (Project, Ground and site, Plan check, Materials and the rest), or right-click it for Move up, Move down, Move to top and Move to bottom.
- **Stacked or Tabs.** Show the panels as a strip of tabs down the edge, one at a time. **Properties** comes forward by itself when you select something. Click the open tab again to fold the panel away and get more room to draw.
- **Dock left or right,** and drag the inner edge to make the panel wider or narrower.
- Your layout is remembered on your computer. **View › Reset panels** puts it back.
- Plan files are unchanged.
