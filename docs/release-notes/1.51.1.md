## New in 1.51.1: the app is now called MIMAR, and hatches zoom with the plan

- **One name everywhere.** The app is now simply **MIMAR**: in the window title, the menu bar, the welcome tour, the start-up splash, the logo, the Windows Start menu and taskbar, the installed web app on iPad, tablets and phones, and on printed sheets, PDFs and exports.
- **No tagline.** The start-up splash shows just the mark and the name, without "Floor plans, drawn to scale".
- **Hatches stay true to the drawing.** On the 2D plan, brick, concrete, tile and the other hatches are now drawn as they would print at 1:100, so zooming in or out makes them bigger or smaller along with the walls instead of staying the same size on screen. Sheets, PDFs and DXF are unchanged.
- Your plans, settings and recent files carry on as before, and the installer and update files keep their names, so updating from 1.51.0 works as usual.
