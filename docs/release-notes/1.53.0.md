## New in 1.53.0: cladding

- **Cladding on walls.** Pick a wall (or several) and choose its **Cladding** in Properties: stone, marble, granite, brick tiles, ceramic or porcelain tiles, terracotta panels, ACP (aluminium composite), glass (spandrel), wood or WPC boards, or textured plaster. Each comes at its usual thickness with its fixing (1½" for stone, 2" for ACP on its frame, 3" for glass, and so on), which you can change. It goes on the wall's outside face, found for you; **Flip face** puts it on the other side.
- **Clad all the outside walls** in one step, from the Structure panel (Frame tab): every floor's outside walls, one undo.
- **In 3D and the elevations** the cladding is a skin over the wall in its own colour and texture, cut round the doors and windows, and the pieces meet neatly round the corners. It covers the structure whatever the facade setting. Click it in 3D to pick its wall, paint it to give it one of your materials, or erase it to take it off.
- **On the plan** it is a band along the wall's face with its own hatch, and in **DXF** on its own layer (A-WALL-CLAD).
- **Setbacks:** cladding is part of the building. Where it reaches past the building line the plan check says so, and you are warned as you clad.
- **Quantities and cost:** the cladding's area by kind, with a starter rate for each (supplied and fixed), and the faces it covers are no longer plastered and painted.
- **IFC:** each wall's cladding is a covering (CLADDING) of its kind.
