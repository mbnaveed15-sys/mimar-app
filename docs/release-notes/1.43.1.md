## New in 1.43.1: Fixes from the 1.43 check-up

- **Dragging an item lines its edge up, not its middle.** Dragging furniture, a column, a slab or any other item now snaps its nearest corner to a wall face, a corner or another item's corner, so a whiteboard or notice board slides along a wall flat against it instead of jumping half into it. Away from everything it moves on the grid, as before.
- **WCs not yet placed are a reminder, not a failure.** A building with toilet rooms but no WCs drawn shows the WCs row as one to check, and it no longer counts as a rule not met.
- **Rows that don't apply say so.** On a building with no stairs, an entrance at ground level or nobody upstairs, the Stair widths, Ramp and Lift rows say "Not needed" and why, instead of "Met".
- **The Checks button's "Plot and setbacks" switch** now also hides each side's own setback row and the local-bylaws rows.
- **Left and right setbacks in the Plot tool:** changing the left side no longer changes the right side as well.
- The Accessible WC's turning circle is now 1.5 m across and sits inside the stall, and an exit message says "1 exit" rather than "1 exits".
