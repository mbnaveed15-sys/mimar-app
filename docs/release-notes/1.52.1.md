## New in 1.52.1: walls and the structure fit together

- **Walls make room for the structure in 3D.** Once the structure is generated, the walls stop at each column's faces, under the beams and round the lintels, so brick and concrete no longer overlap and the frame shows in the walls as it is built. A beam or column narrower than its wall (a 9" beam in a 13½" wall) stays inside the wall.
- **A framing plan for each floor.** The drawings get a sheet per floor (Ground floor framing plan, First floor framing plan...) after the foundation plan, instead of all floors on one sheet. Each shows that floor's columns, which run on down to the footings, and the beams and slab they carry.
