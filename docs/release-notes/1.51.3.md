## New in 1.51.3: the whole building in 3D, checks for each plot, and drawings you can move on sheets

- **The whole model shows solid in 3D.** Floors above the one you are drawing on are no longer faded out, so the 3D view always shows the complete building. To look inside a lower floor, hide the floors above it with the eye in the floor menu.
- **Click an item on any floor to go there.** With the Select tool, clicking a wall, slab, door or anything else in 3D moves you to that item's floor and selects it, with no need to pick the floor from the floor menu first.
- **Moving up and down a floor:** **Page Up** goes to the floor above and **Page Down** to the floor below (on most laptops, **Fn + ↑** and **Fn + ↓**). They are also under **View › Floor above** and **View › Floor below**.
- **Several plots in one plan, each checked on its own.** Draw as many plots as you like side by side. The plan check looks at the plot you are working on: select the plot, or anything on it, and the setbacks, coverage, storeys and the rest are measured against that plot's own bylaws, with only what stands on it. The check says which plot it is ("Plot 2 of 3"), and it stays on that plot until you pick something on another one. The PDF's check page uses the same plot.
- **Move drawings on a sheet.** In the Drawings view, each drawing on a sheet has ← ↑ ↓ → buttons that move it 5 mm (1 mm with Shift), and ⟲ puts it back where the sheet placed it. Moves are saved with the plan and show in the sheet PDF.
