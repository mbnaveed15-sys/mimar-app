## New in 1.43.2: Better corner picking and round shapes in 3D

- **Far corners can be picked in 3D.** Looking along a long wall, its far corner now snaps when you point near it. A corner snaps whenever you can see it, and one hidden behind something else doesn't.
- **Curved walls no longer catch the pointer in 3D.** The joins between the short straight pieces a curved wall is built from no longer count as corners, so you can pick any point along it. Its ends, the corners where it meets other walls and the jambs of its doors and windows still snap.
- **Round things are round in 3D.** Round columns and the round parts of library items (dustbins, basins, urinals, water coolers, wudu taps, WC pans and lab sinks) are drawn as smooth cylinders instead of square blocks, in the 3D view and in the 3D exports. Round columns also come out round in IFC.
