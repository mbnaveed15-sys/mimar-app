## New in 1.40.0: Mimar on the iPad

- **Fits an upright iPad.** Below 1024 px wide (an iPad held in portrait), the menus fold into one **Menu** button, and the side panel becomes a strip of tabs whose panel slides out over the plan and closes with ✕, so the drawing keeps the screen.
- **On-screen buttons for keyboard steps.** Once you use touch, a small bar at the bottom left of the plan gives you **Done** and **Cancel** while drawing, **Flip hinge** while placing a door, and **Delete** and **Deselect** for whatever is selected.
- **Bigger buttons for fingers.** On a touch screen the tools, menu items and switches get larger.
- **Every release now has a full description** on its download page: what is new, how to get Mimar and what it does.
- Plan files are unchanged.
