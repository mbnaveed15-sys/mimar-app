## New in 1.41.0: Curves

- **A Curve tool** on the Draw tool bar (Shift+V). It makes curved **walls** or curved **layout lines**, drawn in any of seven ways:
  - **Arc: ends, then bend.** Click the two ends, then bend it with the pointer or type its radius.
  - **Arc: 3 points.** Click the start, a point the curve passes through, then the end.
  - **Arc: centre, start, end.** Click the centre and the start (or type the radius), then go round and click the end (or type the angle, e.g. 90).
  - **Tangent arc.** Start at the end of a wall and each arc carries on smoothly from the last.
  - **Circle.** Click the centre, then click or type the radius. You get a round wall with the room inside.
  - **Oval.** Click two corners of its box, or type the width and depth (e.g. `20',12'`). You get the oval wall with the room inside.
  - **Freeform curve.** Click points the curve passes through. Enter or a double-click finishes it, and clicking the first point closes it into a room.
- **Rounded rectangles.** The Rectangle tool's **Corners** option can be **Rounded**, with a corner radius.
- **Editing curves:** **Fillet** with a radius now makes one real curved wall instead of short straight pieces. Fillet, **Extend**, **Join** and **Chamfer** work on curved walls and lines. **Push/Pull** thickens a curved wall from its side, or lengthens it round its curve from an end.
- **Curved layout lines** can be bent with their middle grip, given a radius in the side panel, turned into curved walls, and they go to the DXF as true arcs.
- **Snapping to the centre** of any curved wall or line ("Centre of arc").
- Ovals and freeform curves are made of true arcs, so rooms, 3D, doors and windows, quantities and exports all work with them.
- Plan files move to format 12 (for curved layout lines). Older files open as before.
