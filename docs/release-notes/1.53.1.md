## New in 1.53.1: cladding fixes

A check of the new cladding found these, all fixed:

- **No slab band across a clad facade.** The cladding now carries on up over the edge of the slab on the wall, so a two-storey front is one skin instead of stripes of concrete at each floor and at the roof. The strip is measured with the cladding.
- **Cladding on an inside face** (after Flip face) stops at the walls it meets, instead of running through them and showing at the outside corner. The outside cladding next to it still wraps the corner.
- **Inside corners** (an L-shaped house): one piece runs to the wall and the other stops at it, with no gap left in the corner.
- **A wall butting another along its length** (an extension, a garden wall): its cladding stops at that wall's face and cladding.
- **On the plan and in DXF** the cladding band stops at doors and windows instead of running across them.
- **Faster.** Plans with many clad walls redraw about ten times quicker.
- **Picking a clad wall in 3D** now tints its cladding, so you can see what is selected.
- **Sections** hatch cut cladding as its own material (stone, tiles, glass...), not as concrete.
- **Changing the kind** keeps the cladding on the face it was on; **Clad all** puts it on the outside faces.
