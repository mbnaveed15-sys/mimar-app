## New in 1.51.4: each service on its own layer and its own sheet

- **One layer per service, for its points and its wires or pipes.** Electrical, Fans & AC, Water supply, Drainage, Gas, Data & security and Fire safety each have a layer. Turning one off in the Layers panel hides all of that service on the plan, in 3D and in exports: its lights, switches, fittings and every wire or pipe joined to them.
- **Services are no longer part of furniture.** Turning furniture off leaves the services showing, and services are only in the Services library, never in the furniture one. Ceiling fans, AC units, air coolers and AC isolators are now under Fans & AC with the AC wiring, and smoke and heat detectors under Fire safety.
- **The Services library follows the layers.** Its groups are Electrical: lighting, Electrical: switches & sockets, Electrical: power, Fans & AC, Water supply, Drainage, Gas, Data & security and Fire safety, so every item sits under the layer it goes on.
- **A sheet for each service.** The suggested sheets now include an Electrical layout, a Fans & AC layout, a Water supply layout and so on, one for each service the plan has, with each floor that has some of it. A layout shows the building with only that service's points and runs; the ordinary floor plans no longer show services. Any floor's layout can also be added to your own sheets from the Drawings view.
- **Services sit on top of furniture on the plan.** A ceiling light over a bed or a socket behind a sofa is drawn over the furniture, with its wire running underneath it.
- **Services don't upset the furniture checks.** Lights, fans, sockets and other ceiling or wall points, and traps and manholes flush with the floor, no longer count as blocking a bed's, wardrobe's or WC's use zone. Things that really stand on the floor, such as a water tank, pump or outdoor AC unit, still do. Furniture no longer snaps beside service points, and service points line up only with each other.
- **DXF:** AC wiring is on its own layer, M-HVAC-CABL, beside the AC units' M-HVAC.
