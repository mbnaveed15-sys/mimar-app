## New in 1.45.0: CDA, DHA and LDA rules for commercial buildings, and LDA houses made official

- **Bylaws for buildings, not just houses.** A Building project on a CDA, DHA Islamabad/Rawalpindi or LDA plot is now checked against that authority's own rules for its kind of building. Before, the plan check only said "height, coverage and parking: check with the authority".
- **Pick the plot's area.** Select the plot, and an **Area** list under Bylaws says which part of the rules applies. Each use starts on the usual one: offices and shops on a CDA Markaz, schools in an education zone, factories in the industrial area, shops on DHA sector commercial, and so on. CDA has 21 areas (Blue Area, Markaz, Class-III, Community Core, I&T centre, Mauve Area, H-series, the F-5/G-5 public buildings, education zones, the Diplomatic Enclave, the fruit market, hotels, standalone commercial, major avenues, industrial, petrol stations, masjids, hospitals, apartments and halls). DHA has Sector commercial (basement + G+3, 60'), Main commercial (basement + G+5, 82') and amenity plots. LDA has converted plots on commercial roads, civic and commercial centres, the CBD, public building sites, apartments, halls, petrol and CNG stations, bus stands and industry.
- **Type the road's width.** LDA's height zones (low rise to skyscraper) go by the plot's size and the road's width, and so does a CDA major avenue's FAR, so the plot has a **Road width** box. Until it is typed, the plan check asks for it.
- **What the plan check measures for a building:** the FAR (floor area over the plot, without parking, lifts and the mumty), ground and upper-floor coverage, storeys, height, the plot's size and road, the area's setbacks (CDA industrial passes 15' and 15' sides, or 10' and 20'), parking (rooms named Parking, at 250 sq ft a car, against each authority's rule), the veranda along the road, and the mumty. Then the authority's facilities: a lift, emergency stairs, a ramp to the entrance, storey and basement heights, the plinth, shop sizes and stair widths, risers and treads. What the plan can't show (public toilets, ladies' toilets, gaps between blocks) is listed to check.
- **LDA houses are official.** They follow the LDA Building and Zoning Regulations 2019 (with the 2020 amendments and the office order of 28 Oct 2022) instead of a summary. Pick **Approved schemes**, **Built-up areas** or **Open areas** under Bylaws. The plan check now measures coverage, the FAR (with the basement) and the height for each plot size, 3 storeys at most, room and kitchen sizes, the basement and the mumty. A few setbacks change: a 5' rear under 5 marla, one side (not both) from 20 to 30 marla, and 13' rear and sides from 4 kanal. In built-up areas on roads up to 25' wide, the older coverage and FAR and a 38' height apply, and the front is the one you type.
- Plan files move to format 16 (for the plot's area and road width). Older files open as before.
