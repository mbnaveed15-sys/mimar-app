## New in 1.51.8: cleaner drawing sheets and quick repainting

- **The title block names the drawing.** A sheet with one drawing no longer repeats its name and scale under it: the title block along the bottom already gives both. On a sheet of several drawings (sections, elevations, 3D views), each keeps just its name under it, and the title block lists every scale used.
- **Section lines only on floor plans.** The section cut lines (A–A and so on) are drawn on the floor plans, not on the electrical, plumbing and other services layouts.
- **Shift+click to repaint alike (as in SketchUp).** With Paint, Shift+click a wall to give the chosen material to every wall on that floor that has the same material as the one clicked; the same works for rooms, columns, furniture and other items. In 3D, Shift+click on one side of a wall repaints that side of the matching walls. It is one undo step.
