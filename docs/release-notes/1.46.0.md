## New in 1.46.0: phone tools always at hand, a selection bar, touch gestures, floor copies and fixes

- **Pinned tools on a phone.** Select, Move, Eraser and **Fit view** now stay at the start of the tool strip whatever group is open. The group's tools scroll beside them. Pan, Zoom and Orbit moved to the **View** group, because your fingers already pan, zoom and turn the view.
- **Selection bar.** Select something and a bar of buttons appears at the bottom of the plan: **Delete, Duplicate, Rotate 90°, Mirror, Properties** and **Deselect**. It always shows on phones and tablets (icons only where space is short). On a computer, turn it on or off under **View › Selection bar**.
- **Tap with two fingers to undo**, and with three fingers to redo, in 2D and 3D.
- **Twist two fingers in 3D** to turn the model round. In 2D a twist does nothing, so the plan stays the right way up.
- **Flip a door's or window's hinge and swing from the bar.** With a door or a casement window selected, the selection bar has **Flip hinge** and **Flip swing**. Casement windows now have them in Properties and the right-click menu too, and the Window tool has a **Hinge side** (Left or Right) for a casement.
- **Duplicate a floor.** In the floor list, **Duplicate this floor above** adds a new floor on top with a copy of the walls, doors, windows, rooms and furniture of the floor you're on. It leaves out the plot, site items, section lines and roofs.
- **Choose how faint the floor below is.** The floor list has a **Floor below** slider, from Off to fully shown.
- **Fixed: angled corners.** Walls that meet at an angle (a 45° cut corner, say) now join with a clean mitre in the plan, in 3D and in DXF, with no step or notch.
- **Fixed: the Eraser on doors and windows.** Clicking or dragging over a door or window removes just that door or window. Before, it removed the whole wall.
