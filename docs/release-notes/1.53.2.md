## New in 1.53.2: fixes from a whole-app check

Every tool, menu, panel and export was run through, and 14 plans were put through the checks, quantities, drawings, structure and cladding. Nothing crashed; these were found and fixed:

- **Duplicating a floor after generating the structure.** The copy used to take the structure with it, and Regenerate then left those columns and beams alone as if you had drawn them, and Remove structure left them behind. Now the generated structure stays off the new floor, the Structure panel says it is out of date, and **Regenerate** makes it for every floor (columns below grow for the extra storey). Anything you moved, resized or locked is still copied.
- **Columns upstairs stay inside the building line.** Where a wall meets the outside wall on one floor only, that floor's column is turned, and it stood 1½" out past the wall and the building line, so the plan check listed it. The whole column stack now sits flush.
- **No doubled beams** where two walls lie over one another or a hair apart.
- A wording fix in the cladding warning.
