## New in 1.51.10: fixes from the 1.51.9 playtest

### Drawing and editing
- **No more doubled walls.** A rectangle drawn beside a room shares its wall, and a wall drawn along one that is already there adds only the new part.
- **A rectangle over a room makes its own room.** One half over a room splits it; one inside a room (a store in a hall) gets a room of its own.
- **Big arrays no longer freeze the app.** A grid array makes at most 1,000 in all (rows × columns), and the panel says so.
- **Number boxes keep the focus.** Up and Down step the box again and again instead of moving the item. Shift steps 100 mm in metric. A "Mixed" box (items of different sizes) isn't stepped. Ctrl+Z undoes a label drag straight away, and the arrows are bigger on touch screens.
- **The Measurements box clears a refused entry**, so the next typing starts afresh.

### 3D
- **Stairs follow floor heights.** Changing the wall height, a floor's own height, the slab or the plinth keeps each stair climbing its whole floor.
- **A car porch stands on the ground.** Its columns run down to the ground and its floor sits just over the lawn, and so do columns outside the house.
- **Stairwells under two upstairs rooms** are open in both rooms' floors.
- **A flat roof takes a skylight anywhere**, as it is now one face.

### Drawings and exports
- **Title blocks:** a lone 3D view says "Not to scale", an empty sheet no longer says "1:undefined", a one-drawing sheet names its drawing, and on A4 portrait and other narrow paper the scale, date and sheet number fit in smaller text.
- **Drawings that don't fit are left off** rather than drawn over the title block, and a nudged drawing stays inside the frame.
- **Long level names** on sections get room of their own.
- **The quick PDF prints on A1** when A1 is picked.
- **IFC export is valid after "Duplicate this floor above".**

### Checks and planning
- **Room sizes are measured across their narrowest part**: a turned room square to its sides, an L-shaped one by its narrowest leg.
- **The layout generator fits 7 and 10 marla plots.** When the rooms ticked don't fit, it leaves out the car porch (then the drawing room, then a bedroom) and says what it left out.
- **LDA built-up houses** ask for the road width when it isn't typed.
- **A bath light switched from outside the door** is no longer flagged.
- **Rooms named Lawn, Garden, Driveway, Car porch or Garage** count as outdoors, so their items go on the lawn.

### Interface and files
- **On iPads and touch laptops the tool rail fits the window**, wrapping into another column instead of scrolling the page.
- **Two windows no longer overwrite each other's autosave.** Once another window saves, this one stops autosaving and says so: save it to a file, or reload.
- **A .mimar file with no plan in it, or nothing readable, is refused** and the current plan is kept.
- **On a phone, Properties no longer pops up the keyboard.**
