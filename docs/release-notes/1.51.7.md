## New in 1.51.7: real stairs, the mumty, floor or lawn by itself, and services checked

- **Stairs are built as real stairs.** A stair between floors is now a sloping slab with its steps on top, open underneath, and its landings are slabs too, instead of solid blocks standing on the floor. A ramp is a sloping slab, and a curved stair's steps sit on a stepped slab. Steps up the plinth and on the lawn stay solid, as they are built on the ground. Sections, IFC and 3D exports show the same.
- **Stairwell openings drawn properly.** The cross marking a stairwell's opening on the floor above stays inside the opening, so an L-shaped stair no longer has lines running across the room beside it (on the plan, on sheets and in DXF).
- **The mumty stays under its height limit.** A floor named Mumty, Stair tower or Roof (or holding a room named Mumty) now has 7'6" walls by default, 8' with its roof slab, which is under every authority's mumty height. Set the floor's own height to change it.
- **Floor or lawn by itself on Ground.** Once your plan has the lawn level, furniture, services, columns, stairs and blocks placed on Ground go on the floor when they are inside the house and on the lawn when they are outside it. Moving them in or out switches them too. The Floor | Lawn buttons still let you choose by hand.
- **Fans are on the electrics.** Ceiling, exhaust and wall fans and air cooler points are now electrical items, wired on a lighting or power circuit and shown on the electrical layout. The HVAC layer is now called Air conditioning.
- **Services checks.** Every service point takes wires or pipes of its own trade. Once a floor has some of a point's wires or pipes, any point joined to none of them is flagged in the hints.
- **Services layouts show only services.** A trade's layout in Drawings no longer shows furniture or decor, only its own points and runs and the fixtures its pipes join.
