## New in 1.51.6: exact lengths from grips, rooms from walls, and better wall snapping

- **Type a length after using an end grip.** Select a wall, layout line or beam, click (or drag) the grip at one of its ends, then type a length such as `12'6` or `4m` and press Enter. The end you used slides along the line to that length, and the other end stays put. Walls joined at that end follow it, as they do when dragging.
- **Walls that close a loop make a room.** Drawing walls round an area (with the Wall tool, straight or curved) now makes a room there as soon as the loop closes, as the Rectangle tool already did. One Undo takes back the last wall and the room together. A wall drawn across a room splits it in two: the room keeps its name for the part round its middle, and the other part becomes a new room. Areas under 1 m² (shafts, gaps) are left alone.
- **Furniture backs onto the wall nearest the pointer.** Placing or dragging an item near a corner now puts its back against the wall you are pointing beside, instead of whichever wall its far edge happened to come nearest. It still tucks into the corner.
