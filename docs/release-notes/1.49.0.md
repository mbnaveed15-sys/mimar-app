## New in 1.49.0: easier editing, the plinth on the plan, door swings and room minimums, and phone polish

- **Room corner grips.** A selected room has a grip at each corner. Drag it and the walls that meet there move together, so the room (and the room next door) reshapes and keeps its name.
- **Corner grips on furniture and stairs.** Drag a corner and the opposite corner stays where it is. Hold **Shift** to resize about the middle, as before. A beam has square grips on its sides that set its width.
- **Furniture lines up with other furniture.** While placing or dragging, an item sits flush beside another one and lines up with its front, back or middle (a side table beside a bed, chairs in a row). Against a wall it only slides along the wall. Hold **Alt** to place it freely.
- **Change several items at once.** With several walls, furniture items, columns, beams or slabs selected, Properties shows the settings they share (thickness, height, width, depth). A box that says **Mixed** has different values, and what you type goes to all of them in one undo step.
- **Mirror keeps groups.** A mirrored copy of a whole group stays a group. A mirrored component copy becomes a group of its own, named "(mirrored)".
- **The plinth on the plan.** Where the plinth stands out past the ground floor's walls, its outline shows on the plan, in the plan PDF and in DXF (layer A-WALL-PLIN). Click it to select the plinth.
- **Plinth settings per wall.** A ground-floor wall's Properties has **Plinth: As the rest / Its own settings / None**. Its own settings give the plinth under that wall its own projection and material. The height stays one figure, because it is the floor level.
- **Rooms on the floor and the lawn together.** On Ground, the Room tool finds a space closed in by the house's walls and the boundary walls together, such as a courtyard.
- **Door swings count in use zones.** A door that swings into the space a bed, wardrobe or desk needs is flagged in the hints ("a door swings there").
- **Room minimums.** Houses are checked for narrow corridors (900 mm), doors (750 mm), the front door (900 mm) and stairs (900 mm), with the wheelchair figures when that setting is on. **Plan check › Least sizes and where they come from** lists each figure with its source (the ADA Standards or common practice).
- **Use zones in 3D and exports.** The free space furniture needs shows as a light tint on the floor in 3D for selected items and while placing one. A new setting, **Print furniture use zones in PDF and PNG exports**, prints them.
- **Phones and tablets.** On a touch screen the welcome tour talks in taps and teaches the gestures (two-finger tap to undo, three to redo, pinch, the 3D twist). The 3D twist now turns the model round the point between your fingers. On small phones the tool buttons are narrower, so more of a group shows.
- Plan files move to format 19 (for the plinth settings under single walls). Older files open as before.
