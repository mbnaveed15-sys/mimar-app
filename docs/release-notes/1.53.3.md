## New in 1.53.3: phone, tablet and installer check

The phone and tablet layouts were run at seven screen sizes (every panel, every menu item and dialog, structure and cladding by touch), the four release files were checked against their checksums and opened up, and the desktop app was started and put through saving, opening, recent plans and every export. Everything worked; these were tidied:

- **Hints speak of fingers on a tablet or a phone.** "Tap to select" and "Press and hold for more" instead of "Click" and "Right-click", and no talk of the Space key.
- **Bigger buttons for fingers.** The tool group chips on a phone (Draw, Build, Edit and so on), the eye and lock buttons in Layers, the list of drawings and sheets, and the small arrows beside Snap and Checks.
- **The Structure list fits the Frame panel** on a tablet, instead of pushing the panel sideways.
- **A leaner desktop app.** The installer no longer carries a second, unused copy of the libraries the app is built from: 63 MB less on disk once installed, and a slightly smaller download. Nothing changes in how it works or updates.
