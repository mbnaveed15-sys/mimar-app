## New in 1.51.2: hide floors with one click

- **An eye on every floor.** The floor menu (top bar) now has an eye beside each floor. Click it to hide that floor in the 3D view, for example to look into the ground floor without the floors above, and click again to bring it back. A hidden floor's name is greyed out.
- The floor you are drawing on always shows on the 2D plan, and a hidden floor no longer shows faintly under the floor above it.
- Hidden floors are saved with the plan, can be undone, and come back with **Show all hidden**. The floors above a hidden one stay at their usual heights.
