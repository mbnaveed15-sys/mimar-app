## New in 1.51.0: services (electrical, plumbing and more), saved views and more paper

- **Services tool (Shift+Q).** A library of 71 service points at their real sizes, drawn with the usual plan symbols:
  - **Lighting:** ceiling and down lights, pendants, tube lights, and wall, garden, gate and emergency lights.
  - **Switches and sockets:** one-way and two-way switches, switch boards, fan regulators, bell pushes, and 5 A, 15 A, USB and outdoor sockets. Also AC isolators and cooker points.
  - **Power:** distribution board, meter, UPS, solar inverter, changeover, earth pit and door chime.
  - **Fans and AC:** ceiling, exhaust and wall fans; split, window and cassette AC units, with the split's outdoor unit; and cooler points.
  - **Water:** water meter, overhead and underground tanks, pump, electric and gas geysers, valves, taps, mixers, shower heads, hand showers (muslim showers), garden taps, washing machine points and water filters.
  - **Drainage:** floor traps, gully traps, manholes, septic tanks, soakage pits, rainwater outlets, rainwater pipes, vent pipes and clean-outs.
  - **Gas:** gas meters and valves.
  - **Data and security:** TV, data and phone points, Wi-Fi routers, CCTV cameras, intercoms and video door phones.
  - **Fire safety:** smoke and heat detectors, extinguishers, call points and fire bells.

  Wall items snap against walls and stand at the usual height (switches 4', sockets 1', AC units 7'), which you can change. Ceiling items hang under the ceiling.
- **Wire or pipe tool (Shift+U).** Draw wiring and pipes for lighting, power, AC, data, fire alarm, cold and hot water, drainage, rainwater and gas, each in its own colour and line:
  - **Joining:** start on a switch, light, socket, tap, WC, basin or any service point and the run snaps to it. Clicking another item finishes the run there. Joined ends follow their items when they move.
  - **Drawing:** wires are drawn as curves, the way circuits are drawn (or straight, if you prefer). Pipes are straight, with their size marked, and drains show which way they fall.
- **Their own layers.** Services and runs go on Electrical, Fans & AC, Water supply, Drainage, Gas, Data & security and Fire safety layers, so each services drawing can be shown on its own. They also show when furniture is turned off.
- **Services in 3D, DXF and IFC.**
  - **3D:** service points appear at their heights, and pipes as tubes.
  - **DXF:** each trade and each kind of run has its own layer (E-DEVC, E-LITE, E-POWR, P-DOMW, P-SANR, P-GAS, M-HVAC, F-ALRM and more).
  - **IFC:** items export as proper services elements (light fixtures, switches, outlets, sanitary terminals, valves, tanks, pipes and more).
- **Services schedule (CSV).** File › Export services schedule counts each item and measures each kind of wire and pipe, floor by floor.
- **Services hints.** On floors with services, Mimar points out common gaps:
  - wet rooms with no floor trap;
  - rooms with lights but no switch;
  - fixtures with no water or drain pipe;
  - geysers with no hot water pipe;
  - lights not on a circuit;
  - a run joined to an item that doesn't take it;
  - a plan with no distribution board.
- **Saved views on sheets.** **View › Save plan view** keeps the part of the floor on screen. **View › Save 3D view** keeps the 3D camera. Each saved view appears in the Drawings view, where you can rename it, go back to it or delete it. Add it to any sheet:
  - a plan view at any scale;
  - a 3D view as a line drawing or as a coloured picture.
- **More paper.** Sheets can use A0 to A4, B1 to B4, ARCH A to E, Letter, Legal, Tabloid and ANSI C to E, in landscape or portrait, or a **custom size** typed in millimetres. The plan PDF offers the same list.
- Plan files move to format 21. Older files open as before.
