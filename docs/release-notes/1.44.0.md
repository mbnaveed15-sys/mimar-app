## New in 1.44.0: Dimensions, zoom window, the rest of the curves, phones, and first floors from a room list

- **A Dimension tool** (Shift+D, or type DIM). Click two points and then where the dimension line goes: move up or down for a horizontal one, sideways for a vertical one, or pick Aligned to measure along the slope. Tick **Chain** to carry on from the last point, and pick **Radius** to show a curved wall's or line's radius with one click. You can type the distance, or how far off the line goes. A dimension drawn on a wall, a corner or any item **keeps measuring it** as the plan changes. Dimensions have their own layer, print on the drawings and go to DXF as lines and text. (The walls' own length labels are now called **Wall lengths**, in the View menu and under Walls in Layers.)
- **Zoom window and Zoom previous.** The Zoom tool now zooms to a box you drag on the plan, and **Zoom previous** (type ZP, or the new button by the zoom buttons) goes back through the last views.
- **Curved doors.** A door in a curved wall follows the curve: its frame bends in plan, in drawings and in DXF, and the opening and lintel follow it in 3D. Sliding, folding and rolling-shutter doors bend their panels. Single and double swing doors keep a flat leaf, hinged at the ends of the curved frame.
- **Curved slab voids.** Each edge of a slab's void has a bend grip, like the slab's own edges, and 3D, drawings, quantities, IFC and DXF follow the curve. The slab's panel counts curved void edges and can make them straight again.
- **A cut corner on a curved plot side.** A corner plot can now have curved road sides and a splay (cut corner) together. The cut is measured along the curve, and the building line, boundary walls and plan check follow it.
- **Mimar on phones.** On a phone the plan fills the screen. The menus sit behind one Menu button with Undo and Redo beside it, and the tools run along the bottom in groups (Draw, Build, Edit, Annotate, Site, View). Hold a tool to see its name. The side panel becomes a sheet that peeks from the bottom: tap a tab or drag it up to open it half or all the way. Split view is off on phones, and the welcome tour and the drawings fit the screen. Turned on its side, the tool groups fold into a list to leave the plan more room.
- **A first floor from a room list.** Once the ground floor has a stair, the Layout panel can plan the **First floor** too. Pick bedrooms with baths, a TV lounge, and a prayer room, store, laundry or powder room if you want them, then choose one of the plans. They are laid out round the ground floor's stair, which is left open to below, with walls over the ground floor's walls and baths over the baths and kitchen below where they fit. The space left at the front becomes a terrace with a 3' parapet. The plan goes on a new First floor, as ordinary walls, rooms, doors and windows, and one undo takes it back.
- Plan files move to format 15 (for dimensions and curved voids). Older files open as before.
