## New in 1.39.1: rooms, wall joins and the W key

- **Rooms close properly.** A wall that stops on another wall's face, or anywhere inside its thickness, now joins it. Rooms close, and a dividing wall ended this way splits its room.
- **A room never takes the whole plot.** The Room tool ignores the plot's boundary walls. If the walls around your click aren't closed, it says so and circles the loose wall ends so you can see the gap.
- **W draws walls** (L still works), and **Shift+W** places windows.
- Plan files are unchanged.
