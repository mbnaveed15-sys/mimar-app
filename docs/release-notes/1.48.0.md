## New in 1.48.0: furniture that turns and snaps, resizing one side at a time, and one Ground level

- **Turn furniture before you place it.** With the Furniture tool, a see-through copy follows the pointer. Press **V** to turn it a quarter, or use the **Turn left / Turn right** buttons and the **Angle** box in the library. The next item keeps the same angle. On a touch screen a **Turn 90°** button does the same.
- **Furniture snaps to the room's walls.** Near a wall, an item turns its back to it and sits flush against it, and near a corner it tucks into the corner. This works while placing and while dragging. Hold **Alt** to place it freely.
- **Alt flips snapping.** Holding Alt while you draw or drag turns snapping off when it is on, or on when it is off. Alt+click on a door or window still copies its settings.
- **Resize one side at a time.** A selected furniture item, column, stair, slab, block, pad or roof has square grips on its sides. Drag one to move just that side, then type an exact size if you like.
  - **Scale** takes separate factors (`2,1` doubles the width only), and the Scale tool can work on **Width only**, **Depth only** or **Both**.
  - **Stretch** now resizes furniture, columns and stairs caught in its box.
  - Furniture has a **Height** box.
  - **Reset size** puts furniture back to its library size and columns back to the Structure size.
- **One Ground level.** The ground floor and its lawn are now one **Ground** entry in the floor list, shown together. Its **Draw on: Floor | Lawn** switch picks where new items go. An item's Properties can move it between the floor and the lawn.
- **The plinth is its own item.** Click the plinth in 3D (or **Plinth settings** in a ground-floor wall's Properties) to set its height, how far it **stands out past the wall faces**, and its **material**. Delete leaves it off under that wall only. Quantities count the wider plinth.
- **Automatic stairwells.** A stair cuts its opening in the slab above, shaped to the stair type. The opening changes when the stair changes and closes when the stair is removed. 3D, drawings, exports and quantities follow it.
- **Push/Pull snaps** to the corners and faces of other items, so a pulled face lines up with them.
- **Setback marks follow the visible shape.** The plan check marks an item in a setback only when the part you can see is in it, not its bounding box.
- **Snap and Checks buttons.** A click just turns them on or off. Their menus open only from the **▴** arrow beside them, not on hover.
- Plan files move to format 18 (for furniture heights, items on the lawn and the plinth's settings). Older files open as before.
