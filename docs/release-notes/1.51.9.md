## New in 1.51.9: numbered drawings on shared sheets

- **Drawings on a shared sheet are numbered.** When a sheet holds several drawings (sections, elevations, 3D views), each has just a number in a circle under it. The title block lists them in order of number, each with its name and scale (or "Not to scale" for 3D views). A sheet with one drawing still shows nothing under it: the title block names it and gives its scale.
