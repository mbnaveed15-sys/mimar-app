## New in 1.43.0: Furniture for every building, checks everywhere, left and right setbacks, and exact corners

- **A furniture library for every kind of building.** About 60 new items at real sizes: desks, workstations, meeting tables, reception desks, filing cabinets and lockers for offices; student and teacher desks, whiteboards, lab benches and library shelves for schools; examination couches, hospital beds, waiting seats and a wheelchair space for clinics and hospitals; shelving, clothes rails, display fridges, checkouts and fitting rooms for shops; prayer spaces, a minbar, wudu taps and shoe racks for mosques; café tables, booths and serving counters for restaurants; urinals, WC cubicles, an accessible WC, basin rows and baby change for public toilets; and water coolers, vending machines, notice boards, dustbins and a motorbike. Home gets a dressing table, bunk bed, study desk, bookshelf and washing machine. Each one has its plan symbol, a 3D shape and the free space it needs (from the ADA standards where they give one).
- **A tidier library.** Items are in groups (Home, Office, School, Health, Shop, Prayer, Food, Toilets, Garden & plants, General), each with folding headings. The group that fits your project opens first, a search box finds any item by name, and your last few picks sit in a Recent row.
- **Checks for every kind of building and every locality.** Building projects now have the plan check on from the start, with a row for each building-use check (exits, stairs, corridors, doors, travel distance, WCs, accessible toilet, ramp, lift), as well as their plot's own setbacks. A plot with no authority picked is still checked against its own setbacks. Where Mimar's table for a locality doesn't have a figure (LDA's height, say), the check shows a row to check with the authority instead of leaving it out.
- **More building uses:** hospital, restaurant or café, hotel or hostel, apartments, wedding or community hall, factory or workshop, and warehouse, each with its own people-per-area and WC figures, and wider corridors for hospitals.
- **Left and right side setbacks.** The plot's setbacks are now Front, Rear, Left side and Right side (as seen from the road), in the Plot tool and in the plot's properties, with a button to swap left and right.
- **Every corner can be picked exactly.** Drawing and dragging now snap to the corners of every item (slabs and voids, columns, beams, stairs, furniture, blocks, roofs, plots, and door and window jambs), to the true inside and outside corners where walls meet, and corners win over a nearby midpoint. In 3D, pointing near a corner you can see takes that corner, at its own height. Dragged grips snap the same way.
- **More grips:** drag a corner of a slab, levelled area, block or roof, either end of a beam, or a column's corner to resize it.
- Plan files move to format 14 (for the new furniture and building uses). Older files open as before.
