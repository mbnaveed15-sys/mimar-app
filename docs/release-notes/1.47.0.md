## New in 1.47.0: a Ground level, your own plinth, and slabs of any shape

- **A Ground (site) level.** The floor list now has **Ground (site)** under the Ground floor (after a basement, if you have one). Its floor is the lawn. Every tool works on it, and what you draw stands on the ground: plinth walls, low garden walls, platforms, steps and ramps up the plinth, planters. New walls drawn there rise from the lawn to the ground floor, and stairs climb the same height. The Ground floor shows faintly over it, and its wall corners snap, so the plinth is easy to draw under the walls.
- **Lawn and plinth heights in the floor list.** With Ground (site) picked, the floor list shows the **Lawn height** (what the plot is levelled to) and the **Plinth height** (the ground floor's level), the same settings as in Ground and site and in Settings.
- **Draw your own plinth.** Pick **Plinth: Draw my own** and ground-floor walls start at the floor instead of running down to the ground, so the plinth is whatever you draw on Ground (site), stepped or raised as you like. **Automatic** works as before. Quantities count the plinth walls you draw, with their foundations, in place of the automatic band.
- 3D, sections and elevations, quantities and cost, IFC, DXF and PDF all follow it. The Ground (site) level adds no floor area, storey or slab to the plan check, and a ramp drawn on it counts as the ramp to the entrance.
- **Slabs of any shape.** The Slab tool has four ways to draw, on the right:
  - **Rectangle**, as before (or click inside walls to cover that room).
  - **Corner by corner**: click the corners of any shape, and click the first corner (or press Enter) to finish. Ctrl+Z takes back a corner.
  - **Rooms**: click the rooms to cover (click again to drop one), then press Enter for one slab over them all. It reaches the outside walls' outer faces and stops halfway over a wall shared with a room you left out. A courtyard in the middle stays open.
  - **Follow the walls**: one click inside the walls covers them, whatever their shape, out to their outside faces.
  - The edges can still be bent into curves with the grips.
- Properties shows a wall's or column's usual height for its own floor.
- Plan files move to format 17 (for the Ground (site) level and your own plinth). Older files open as before.
