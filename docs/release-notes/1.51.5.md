## New in 1.51.5: the Array tool, and smoother editing

- **Array (Shift+I, under Modify).** Select what to copy (or just click it), then:
  - **Rows & columns:** set how many columns and rows in the panel, click a base point, then click where the next copy goes (one cell across and down) or type the spacing, e.g. `@4',-3'`. With one row (or one column) the copies run in a line at any angle.
  - **Round a centre:** set how many in all and the angle to fill (360° for a full circle), click the centre, then click or press Enter. The copies can turn round with the circle or keep facing the same way.
  - **Along a path:** set how many, then click a wall, layout line, wire or pipe: the copies are spaced evenly from end to end, turning with the path if you like.
  - Every copy is an ordinary item, and the panel stays in view while things are selected.
- **Copy keeps copying.** The Copy command (type CO) now works as in AutoCAD: after each copy the next one hangs on the pointer from the same base point, until Esc. Ctrl with Move still makes one copy, and 3x or /3 still repeat it.
- **Dragging a wall keeps its corners.** Drag a wall, or the grip at its end, and the walls meeting it there stretch with it, so the room stays closed and its area updates.
- **Esc does more:**
  - Esc while dragging something puts it back where it was.
  - Esc in a side-panel field leaves the field, so the next key picks a tool again.
  - With nothing left to cancel or select, Esc goes back to the Select tool.
- **Mirror** turns furniture the right way: a bed mirrored across the room lands with its headboard on the opposite wall, not facing into the room.
- **Services fixes:**
  - A mirrored copy of a wire or pipe joins the copies of its items, and a mirrored drain still falls the right way.
  - Moving a switch or socket up or down starts from its own mounting height.
  - A mounting height of 0 is kept when the plan is saved.
  - Clicking a light drawn over a bed picks the light.
  - Underground tanks and pits can be set to any depth.
- **Smaller things:**
  - Erasing a locked item no longer erases the room under it; it says the item is locked.
  - Move, Rotate and Mirror say "click the base point" when something is already selected.
  - Page Up with no floor above says how to add one.
  - The Properties heading names every item with a capital letter.
